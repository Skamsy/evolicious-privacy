Privacy Policy - Evolicious Mobile

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Evolicious Mobile for Android (package com.evolicious.game) is developed by Ben Hansen. This policy describes the mobile game, not any separate PC or Steam version.

Game information stored on your device

No account is required. The game stores progress, unlocks, currency, preferences and your run history on your device. Run records include gameplay statistics, dates, character builds and images of your in-game creature. These images are generated by the game; the app does not access your camera or photo library. Run identifiers are randomly generated game-record identifiers, not hardware identifiers.

The developer does not operate a server that receives your saved progress or run history. The mobile My Runs screen shows your locally saved runs and does not upload them to Steam or an online leaderboard.

Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

The game does not include advertising or account sign-in. Unity Analytics, Unity Engine Diagnostics, Unity crash reporting and Unity performance reporting are disabled in this release. The game does not use these services to send gameplay analytics or crash reports to the developer or Unity.

Google Play and Android services

The game uses Google Play Asset Delivery to install its game assets. Google describes this service as processing device metadata and the application version to deliver the correct assets and maintain them after updates. Google states that this data is encrypted, is not shared with third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period. This processing is distinct from the game's locally stored run history. See Google's Asset Delivery disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy .

Android and Google Play may independently process installation information and, depending on your system settings, usage and crash or ANR diagnostics. This platform processing is governed by your platform settings and Google's policies; disabling Unity diagnostics does not disable your phone's system diagnostics.

Backup, retention and deletion

The app is configured to exclude its saved data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. It does not provide cloud-save recovery. Device manufacturers and tools outside the app may have their own migration behavior.

Local game data remains until it is removed. You can clear it using Android Settings > Apps > Evolicious Mobile > Storage > Clear data (wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. This deletes progress and run history and cannot be undone through the developer. We do not promise a fixed automatic retention period for local runs.

External websites

If you choose to open a credit/source link, it opens an external website. The game does not append your run history to that link. The website and your browser handle information under their own policies. This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; website hosting may process technical request information separately from the game. See GitHub's Privacy Statement .

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information or game files unless needed for your request. Contact benhansendev@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests concerning information you supplied directly.

Children's privacy

The game has no account or advertising features and does not intentionally request personal information from children. If you believe personal information from a child has been sent to the developer, please contact the address above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the game's features or data practices change. The date above will identify the latest revision.
