Privacy Policy - Evolicious

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Evolicious is developed by Ben Hansen. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information Collection

Evolicious does not collect, store, or share personal information. No account is required to play.

Game Data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Third-Party Services

Evolicious does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not retain personal data. Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children's Privacy

Evolicious does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change.
